Padhaus is a fair-launch launchpad with no gatekeepers and no surprises. Anyone can launch a token on a transparent bonding curve — and the rules you launch with are the rules your token keeps, forever.

Free to launch

There is no launch fee. You pay network gas only — creating a token costs nothing but the transaction itself.

Your fee, your call

Set your own trade fee at launch, anywhere from 0% to 5%. Whatever you choose is split 50/50 between you, the creator, and the platform.

Frozen at launch

Your fee is locked into the token the moment it launches. It can never be raised, lowered, or changed unilaterally — by anyone, including us.

Built to graduate

Tokens trade on a bonding curve. When the curve completes, the token graduates to a DEX liquidity pool. A flat 2% graduation fee applies only at that moment.
